My work

My contributions

Three ways I’ve shown up for children and communities — written in my own voice, without inflated metrics or invented proof.

Libraries

I helped set up and support libraries so children in underserved communities could access books and a quiet place to learn. My work sat alongside broader education initiatives run by the organizations I volunteered with — building spaces where curiosity has a home.

Donation drives

I organized donation efforts — collecting and coordinating contributions of books, essentials, and other materials that communities needed. These drives were about turning goodwill into something tangible that reached children and families on the ground.

Fundraising campaigns

I hosted fundraising campaigns for underprivileged children — bringing people together around a cause, spreading the word, and helping channel support toward education and welfare. The goal was simple: more resources for kids who need them most.

Partners

Organizations

Background on the NGOs I’ve worked with. Figures below are from each organization’s public materials — they describe organizational reach, not my individual KPIs.

Prashraya Welfare Foundation

Formerly Team Aashraya (Ashraya)

A youth-led Section 8 non-profit that began in 2019 as Team Aashraya in Tumda, Madhya Pradesh, and evolved into Prashraya Welfare Foundation. Focus areas include Shiksha (education & libraries), Shakti (women), Paryavaran, Chikitsa, and Vastra. Founded by Priyanshu Khemariya.

Public materials describe 400+ libraries established & supported, learning centres, Aashraya Vidyalayas, and presence across 20+ states — organizational claims, not personal metrics.

Visit prashraya.org →

Hamari Pahchan NGO

Also stylized Hamari Pehchan

A Delhi-based NGO active since around 2015, working across education, skill development, hunger relief, menstrual health, and related community programs. Flagship initiatives include Drishti (education), Sukhad, and various skill-development programs.

Their work centers on identity, opportunity, and support for communities that are often overlooked — aligning closely with education and child welfare themes.

Visit hamaripahchan.org →

Thread

How this work connects

Libraries without books, or children without the means to stay in school, leave gaps that no single classroom can fill. My volunteer work — setting up and supporting libraries, organizing donations, and hosting fundraising campaigns — was aimed at one thread: education access for underprivileged children.

Each activity feeds the next: spaces to learn, materials to fill them, and community support to keep those efforts alive. That’s the impact story I can own honestly — contribution, not inflated certificates.
